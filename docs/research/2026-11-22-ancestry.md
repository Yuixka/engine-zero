# November 22: root-ancestry leakage audit

## Problem
The seeded search imports a hardcoded, previously optimized four-bar. Mutations of this incumbent previously received `origin: generated`, even though their root was not an independent random candidate. A benchmark reporting these as independently discovered designs would overstate discovery evidence.

## Hypothesis and falsification
**H1:** Explicit root ancestry propagated through every mutation can distinguish incumbent-descended from independently rooted generated designs. For fixed seed 716, test that the original incumbent and its descendants retain `incumbent-derived`, imported designs retain `imported-derived`, fresh factory candidates have `generated-root`, and benchmark champion ancestry counts sum to all champions. A missing or mismatched label falsifies the implementation claim.

## Scope and prior art
Data provenance, evolutionary lineages and train/test leakage audits are established. This implementation is bookkeeping, not a novel invention. It does not assert that generated-root designs are new to humanity or statistically independent. Root ancestry is not a full mutation DAG; future work should store parent IDs and compare designs to external literature.

## Next experiment
Run a matched-evaluation-budget comparison excluding incumbent-derived candidates from discovery rankings, with >=10 predeclared seeds and external held-out objectives, plus independent physical feasibility checks. Report failures and missing families. No 3D physics or fabrication validation yet.
